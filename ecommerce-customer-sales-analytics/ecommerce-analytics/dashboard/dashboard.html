Finding — Apparel & Clothing and Footwear return at roughly double the platform average, driven mostly by fit — and significantly delayed orders return at a noticeably higher rate than on-time ones. Recommendation — add size guidance / fit quizzes on the highest-return apparel listings, and prioritize carrier reliability on Standard shipping, which has the highest delay rate of the three tiers.
